# Product naming decision history

Review dated **2026-10-30 (Asia/Shanghai)**. Initially **Cogito** was accepted only as an internal codename while product development continued, without changing assemblies, namespaces, data directories or package identifiers.

First party sources showed existing uses: Cogito Studio as a cross platform desktop AI workspace; mudler/cogito as a Go agent/workflow library; Deep Cogito's continuing model brand including v2.1 dated 2025-11-19; and Cogito/Verint's contact center AI. These were naming/search differentiation evidence, not this project's functional acceptance of those products or a legal trademark opinion.

No trademark clearance or domain/repository availability was established. USPTO search/TSDR guidance was viewed, but no reliable current case status was extracted. A reference to application 99711894 timed out and was explicitly excluded as confirmed rights evidence. Search results, domains and open source licenses do not establish trademark permission.

A later same day user authorized public naming adopted **Cogitide** (Cogito + tide). Exact name web/GitHub searches did not find an identical repository in that snapshot; this supported the selected public project name, not trademark registration or exclusive rights. Display branding/repository changed, while Workbench assemblies/data/native credential service identifiers stayed compatible. Earlier Cogito references remain historical.

This English page summarizes the preserved source. [The full Chinese record](naming.zh-CN.md) retains complete historical details, examples, evidence paths and qualifications. Historical dates and test counts above are not new verification claims.
